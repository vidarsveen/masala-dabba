# Kerala regional review

Completed 22 September 2026 as one four-reading bilingual batch. This ledger records the
research, conservation decisions, dependent-content reconciliation and verification used for
the internal regional gate. It does not authorize recording or publication.

## Sources and factual decisions

- The Spices Board of India [identifies Kerala and Idukki as major centres for small cardamom](https://www.indianspices.com/sites/default/files/Invitation%20to%20BSM%20at%20Kattapana%2C%2023%20Oct%202025.pdf).
  The course retains pepper and cardamom as the regional spine while removing changing production
  rankings and the claim that no comparable growing environment existed elsewhere.
- FAO's [Kuttanad Below Sea Level Farming System](https://www.fao.org/agroecology/in-action/detail/kuttanad-below-sea-level-farming-system/en)
  supports the account of rice cultivation behind bunds in reclaimed delta wetlands. The revised
  reading describes the system directly rather than using a global uniqueness superlative.
- Kerala Tourism's [sadya overview](https://www.keralatourism.org/faq/what-is-kerala-sadya-and-what-is-special-about-it)
  and [Onasadya account](https://www.keralatourism.org/onam/ona-sadya/sadya) support a vegetarian
  banana-leaf feast, common dishes and recognisable serving conventions. They also show regional
  variation, so fixed item counts, universal placement, leaf-folding verdicts and participation by
  every religious household were withdrawn.
- The USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service states that
  [seafood should not remain out for more than two hours and cooked fish keeps three to four days
  under refrigeration](https://ask.fsis.usda.gov/article/How-long-can-you-store-fish). Kudampuli is
  no longer presented as a room-temperature preservative. The reading, recipe, quiz, glossary and
  spice card now require prompt chilling for next-day curry.
- The Metropolitan Museum's [Indian Ocean trade account](https://resources.metmuseum.org/resources/metpublications/pdf/The_Year_One_Art_of_the_Ancient_World_East_and_West.pdf)
  supports Malabar pepper's importance in Roman-era trade. UNESCO's
  [Indian Ocean exchange paper](https://whc.unesco.org/document/135363) describes Pattanam as
  evidence strengthening the Muziris hypothesis, so the course no longer calls the identification
  settled.
- Kerala Tourism's [Kappad account](https://www.keralatourism.org/malabar/kappad-beach/91) records
  both the traditional landing site and disagreement over the exact shore. Its accounts of
  [China–Kerala trade](https://www.keralatourism.org/faq/did-china-have-trade-links-with-kerala-hundreds-of-years-ago)
  support early fifteenth-century contact without proving one origin route for the Fort Kochi nets.

## Changes and conservation record

| Reading | Main corrections | Material retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Removed changing pepper rankings, sole-pre-chilli-heat and universal pantry claims; replaced a false room-temperature kudampuli rule | Monsoon geography, pepper processing, cardamom picking, curry leaf, Kerala souring ingredients and dish-specific blends |
| 2 | Replaced single-staple and three-meals-a-day generalisations; qualified parboiling, toddy and coconut-milk rules; made meal structure variable | Matta rice, appam, idiyappam, puttu, coconut forms and Kuttanad agriculture |
| 3 | Added safe fish cooling; separated food practice from religion percentages; qualified ishtu ownership, sadya layout and leaf-folding etiquette | Meen curry, karimeen, ishtu, avial, parotta and beef fry, sadya and payasam |
| 4 | Made Pattanam a Muziris candidate; qualified mosque, Christian, fishing-net and voyage traditions; replaced single-route crop histories | Roman pepper trade, Arab, Jewish, Christian and Chinese connections, Vasco da Gama, American crops and colonial trade |

The English fact and listener passes removed unsafe storage advice, origins, superlatives and
statewide household rules. The Norwegian-first pass removed categorical translations and
time-sensitive Oslo shop claims. Bilingual comparison confirmed safety instructions, dates,
recipe quantities, list order, image keys and vegetarian flags.

Quizzes, glossary entries and spice cards now carry the same qualifications. The meen curry recipe
requires shallow-container refrigeration within two hours and thorough reheating. Appam batter is
refrigerated after fermentation if it is not cooked immediately, toddy is limited to regulated
food-grade sources, and chicken stew reaches 74 °C.

# Andhra and Telangana regional review

Completed 22 September 2026 as one four-reading bilingual batch. This ledger records the
research, conservation decisions, dependent-content reconciliation and verification used for
the internal regional gate. It does not authorize recording or publication.

## Sources and factual decisions

- The Indian GI Registry entries for [Guntur Sannam Chilli](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/143)
  and [Hyderabad Haleem](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/193)
  establish the protected names, areas and 2010 registration records. The course keeps both
  distinctions while removing changing production rankings and an unsupported claim that haleem
  was available only for one month.
- The Hyderabad district administration's [Charminar record](https://hyderabad.telangana.gov.in/tourist-place/charminar/)
  supports Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah and the 1591 date. The page records several explanations for
  the monument, so the course no longer presents one founding legend or a water-shortage account
  as settled fact.
- The district's [history of Hyderabad](https://hyderabad.telangana.gov.in/about-district/) and
  [Telangana Tourism's city account](https://tourism.telangana.gov.in/page/hyderabad) support the
  1591 foundation and planned urban core. The revised reading distinguishes that firm chronology
  from disputed motives and later diamond provenances.
- The US government's [FoodSafety.gov account of *Bacillus cereus*](https://www.foodsafety.gov/food-poisoning/bacteria-and-viruses)
  identifies cooked rice and other food held too long at room temperature as sources of illness
  and calls for shallow containers and refrigeration within two hours. Pulihora no longer carries
  a day-without-refrigeration claim.
- The FDA's [2024 Food Code supplement](https://www.fda.gov/media/183271/download) explains that
  flavouring cooked rice with acid does not remove temperature control unless a measured process
  reaches the required equilibrium pH. Tamarind is therefore not treated as a safety control.
- The National Center for Home Food Preservation's
  [garlic-in-oil guidance](https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/freeze/vegetable/freezing-garlic-in-oil/)
  documents botulism risk in room-temperature oil mixtures. The old course recipe had no measured
  pH, water activity or validated process, so its year-long room-temperature avakaya instructions
  were withdrawn. The replacement is a small garlic-free refrigerator batch with discard guidance.

a heavy blade. Pulihora is served promptly or chilled within two hours.

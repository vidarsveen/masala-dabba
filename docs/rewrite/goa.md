# Goa and Konkan regional review

Completed 22 September 2026 as one four-reading bilingual batch. This ledger records the
research, conservation decisions, dependent-content reconciliation and verification used for
the internal regional gate. It does not authorize recording or publication.

## Sources and factual decisions

- [Government of Goa, history of Goa](https://dip.goa.gov.in/history-of-goa/) supports the 1510
  conquest and the end of Portuguese rule in 1961. The readings now call this one of Asia's
  longest European colonial tenures rather than making an unsupported absolute claim.
- [Government of Goa, people of Goa](https://dip.goa.gov.in/people-of-goa/) and
  [Incredible India, Goan food and cuisine](https://www.prod.incredibleindia.gov.in/content/incredible-india-v2/en/destinations/goa/food-and-cuisine.html)
  support a cuisine formed through Konkan ingredients, several religious communities and
  Portuguese contact. The course no longer divides Goa into two sealed kitchens or presents one
  fish-curry plate as universal.
- [Goa Tourism's visitor directory](https://goatourism.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/Goa-Tourist-Directory-2019.pdf)
  supports rice, fish, coconut, kokum, vinegar and the named dishes as recognisable parts of Goan
  food. Household variation is now explicit throughout.
- [Government of Goa, Ponda](https://www.goa.gov.in/places/ponda/) supports Ponda's temple
  landscape. Claims about temple displacement now distinguish documented pressure and movement
  from a single universal migration story.
- [Goa University research on Portuguese language policy and the Inquisition](https://irgu.unigoa.ac.in/drs/bitstream/handle/unigoa/3940/pereira_j_g_2010.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)
  supports the 1560–1812 Inquisition chronology, its 1774–1778 suspension and the 1684 decree
  against Konkani. The revised reading avoids claiming uniform enforcement across all territory.
- [Scholarship on Garcia da Orta's 1563 Goa printing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7939634/)
  establishes that American chilli was present on India's west coast by then. The course retains
  the Portuguese maritime route while dropping the unprovable claim that Goa was the exact first
  landing place of every Indian chilli.
- [Ministry of Food Processing Industries, Goa profile](https://mofpi.gov.in/sites/default/files/KnowledgeCentre/State%20Profile/Goa.pdf)
  supports khazan agriculture and cashew processing. Korgut is now described as one local rice
  variety associated with saline conditions, and the invented cashew soil-binding explanation
  has been removed.

## Changes and conservation record

| Reading | Main corrections | Material retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Qualified the chilli route, kept long pepper in continued use, varied chilli heat and recheado recipes, removed room-temperature meat and months-long paste-storage claims, and replaced a religious binary with overlapping traditions | Portuguese maritime exchange, palm vinegar, chilli, recheado, chouriço and xacuti |
| 2 | Made xit kodi a meal pattern rather than a fixed daily plate, varied ukda and khazan rice, allowed different fish-curry and solkadhi methods, and removed blanket community food rules | Rice, coconut, fish, kokum, khazan farming, sannas and solkadhi |
| 3 | Corrected the <em>vinha d'alhos</em> etymology without forbidding potato, varied xacuti, replaced repeated room-temperature reheating with refrigerated rest, and treated cafreal, balchão and bebinca histories carefully | Vindaloo, xacuti, sorpotel, cafreal, balchão, sannas and layered bebinca |
| 4 | Shortened colonial chronology, qualified temple displacement and language enforcement, removed two sealed-cuisine and cashew soil-binding stories, and treated pav and curry etymologies as qualified links | 1510–1961 chronology, Ponda temples, Konkani, cashew feni, bread and migration |

The quizzes now test qualified distinctions and safe storage. Glossary entries describe recipe
families and refrigerated handling rather than fixed spice counts or preservation by vinegar and
oil. Spice cards separate flavour from food safety. Recipes identify their own methods as versions,
allow potato in vindaloo, vary xacuti blends and bebinca layer counts, and correct Bebinca's
vegetarian flag. The bilingual regional introduction now centres Konkan ingredients, communities
and exchange without claiming that chilli first landed in Goa or that one plate represents every
household.

## Review and verification

The English fact pass removed absolutes, unsupported origin stories, invented mechanisms and unsafe
storage guidance. The listener pass shortened all four readings and removed catalogue-style runs.
The Norwegian-first pass replaced literal and categorical constructions while preserving the same
uncertainty. The bilingual alignment pass confirmed dates, dish distinctions, community terms,
storage guidance and list order.

Prose lint leaves only inspected rhythm heuristics, with every sentence at or below the 35-word
ceiling. Wiring, quiz, spice, recipe and credit checks pass, as do all seven offline transfer tests.
The site build completes at 19.7 MB with no recordings in the worktree. The freshness check reports
Goa's eight revised scripts stale, bringing the expected course total to 58. Recording remains
pending with the course-wide audio stage, and no existing binary was overwritten.

# Awadh regional review

Completed 22 September 2026 as one four-reading bilingual batch. This ledger records the
research, conservation decisions, dependent-content reconciliation and verification used for
the internal regional gate. It does not authorize recording or publication.

## Sources and factual decisions

- [Development Commissioner MSME, Kannauj perfume cluster](https://www.dcmsme.gov.in/tcsp/Program%20Overview/Kannauj_V1.pdf)
  documents the traditional deg-bhapka distillation process, clay-and-cloth sealing and the use
  of sandalwood oil as a carrier. The reading now distinguishes this perfume tradition from the
  food-grade rose and kewra products used in cooking.
- [Government of Odisha, Ganjam district investor guide](https://ganjam.odisha.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-03/District%20Investors%20Guide_%20Ganjam.pdf)
  attributes about 90 percent of India's kewda-flower production to Ganjam. Exact flower counts
  for producing kewra water were removed because they were not supported consistently.
- [Odisha Review, geographical indications](https://magazines.odisha.gov.in/Orissareview/2020/Apr-May/engpdf/GIs.pdf)
  confirms the Ganjam kewda flower and Ganjam kewda rooh registrations and supports retaining the
  district's importance without turning all kewra products into one process.
- [Government of Uttar Pradesh, Bara Imambara](https://nri.up.gov.in/article/en/bara-imambara)
  supports the 1784 construction under Asaf-ud-Daula and its role as famine employment. The story
  that workers built by day and nobles dismantled by night remains explicitly identified as a
  tradition rather than documented fact.
- [Journal of Food Science, papain and meat tenderisation](https://ift.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1365-2621.1974.tb17985.x)
  supports papaya protease as a tenderiser. The kebab account now explains the mechanism while
  warning that excess enzyme can make meat pasty; the toothless-nawab origin remains folklore.
- [Encyclopaedia Britannica, Awadh](https://unvis.it/www.britannica.com/EBchecked/topic/673406/Awadh)
  supports the region's rich alluvial plain, the 1856 British annexation and the annexation's role
  in grievances preceding the 1857 uprising. The course now calls misgovernment the East India
  Company's allegation and acknowledges its political interests.
- [British Library, Wajid Ali Shah letter](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/?f%5Bproject_collections_ssim%5D%5B%5D=India+Office+Private+Papers&f%5Brelated_names_ssim%5D%5B%5D=East+India+Company%2C+1600-1858&page=7&per_page=50&sort=date)
  catalogues his January 1857 protest to Queen Victoria. It supports presenting the annexation as
  contested rather than repeating the Company's justification as neutral fact.
- [Ministry of External Affairs, India Perspectives](https://www.mea.gov.in/Images/amb1/IP_Jun_July_2019_Book.pdf)
  records Wajid Ali Shah's household and Awadhi cooks at Metiabruz. The reading now follows that
  migration through his death in 1887 without claiming that every Kolkata innovation began there.
- [Sahapedia, Kolkata biryani](https://www.sahapedia.org/the-kolkata-biryani-culture-identity-and-politics)
  documents competing explanations for the potato in Kolkata biryani. The course retains the
  Lucknow-Metiabruz connection and states clearly that the potato's origin story is disputed.

## Changes and conservation record

| Reading | Main corrections | Material retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Distinguished food-grade culinary aromas from perfumery attar, corrected kewda geography, removed unsupported flower counts and qualified universal spice rules | Kannauj distillation, rose, kewra, saffron, cardamom and the potli technique |
| 2 | Explained what sealing changes without claiming it stops evaporation, treated pakki and kacchi as common associations rather than absolute borders, and removed a false causal claim about sheermal | Dum pukht, layered rice, sheermal, rumali roti and the shared dastarkhwan |
| 3 | Marked the toothless-nawab story as folklore, narrowed korma rules, qualified nihari's etymology and breakfast history, and added safe handling for chilled dairy | Galouti and Kakori kebabs, biryani, nihari, malai makhan and shahi tukda |
| 4 | Separated documented famine employment from the day-and-night legend, qualified kitchen ranks, reframed annexation and made the Kolkata-potato story explicitly disputed | Bara Imambara, Lucknow's court, 1856 and 1857, Metiabruz and the cuisine's continuing life |

Quizzes, spice cards, glossary entries and affected recipe notes now carry the same qualifications
in both languages. The pakki and kacchi biryani notes allow regional variation; the nihari recipe
adds prompt chilling and thorough reheating; and the bilingual introduction links courtly restraint,
shared dining, migration and present-day cooking without reducing the region to its former court.

## Review and verification

The English fact pass removed unsupported quantities, universal rules and overconfident origin
stories. The listener pass named subjects in the spoken prose and shortened dense catalogues. The
Norwegian-first pass replaced literal constructions, checked Indian terms in context and preserved
the food-safety qualifications. The bilingual alignment pass confirmed that historical uncertainty,
method variations, dish names and list order match across languages.

Prose lint leaves only inspected rhythm heuristics, with every sentence at or below the 35-word
ceiling. Structure, supporting-content, audio-freshness and transfer-test results are recorded in
the regional milestone in the transfer plan. Recording remains pending with the course-wide audio
stage, and no existing binary was overwritten.

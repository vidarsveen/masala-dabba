# Rajasthan regional review

Completed 21 September 2026 as one four-reading bilingual batch. This ledger records the
research, conservation decisions, dependent-content reconciliation and verification used for
the internal regional gate. It does not authorize recording or publication.

## Sources and factual decisions

- [Government of Rajasthan, geographic profile](https://rc.rajasthan.gov.in/gographicprofile.aspx)
  gives the state's area as 342,239 square kilometres, 10.4 percent of India, and places most
  of the Thar within Rajasthan. The reading now uses those bounded claims.
- [Government of Rajasthan revenue overview](https://hcmripa.rajasthan.gov.in/UploadDoc/Download_Doc/e41aabef-2844-4632-b9c8-1c4aab480e8f-Sources%20Of%20Revenue.pdf)
  reports 1.15 percent of India's surface-water resources. This replaced the loose "near one
  percent" claim and the deterministic claim that every unusual dish follows from scarcity.
- [Rajasthan Tourism, Chand Baori](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/chand-baori.html) and
  [Archaeological Survey of India, Jaipur Circle](https://asijaipurcircle.nic.in/Chand%20Baori.html)
  support the early-medieval date and roughly 3,500 steps. The unsupported thirteen-storey
  formulation was removed.
- [ICAR, Indian Farming, January 2023](https://icar.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-05/Indian-farming-January-2023.pdf)
  and [ICAR crop cultivar guide](https://icar.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-06/Crop-Cultivars-2nd-Edition.pdf)
  support pearl millet's heat and drought tolerance and early cultivars maturing in roughly
  65 to 90 days. Claims that no other important cereal can do this were removed.
- [ICAR on moth bean](https://icar.gov.in/en/node/3850) supports describing it as drought-hardy,
  not as India's single most drought-tolerant pulse.
- [GI Registry, Bikaneri Bhujia](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/142)
  confirms the registered geographical indication. The false account of sprouted beans becoming
  fried strands was corrected: moth and gram flours are made into seasoned dough and extruded.
- [GI Registry, Mathania Mirchi application](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/1490)
  records an application under examination, not an established GI. The course now treats heat as
  variable and describes the chilli's colour and aroma without promising that a dozen pods are mild.
- [ICAR/Indian Journal of Small Ruminants, kachri tenderisation](https://indianjournals.com/article/ijsr-25-1-016)
  and [Phytochemistry protease study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0031942206001191)
  support kachri's protease activity and traditional use as a meat tenderiser.
- [Rajasthan Tourism, Rajasthani cuisine](https://www.tourism.rajasthan.gov.in/rajasthani-cuisine.html)
  supports laal maas as a curd-and-spice meat dish associated with royal cooking. Hunting-camp
  origins remain culinary tradition and are now presented with that limitation.
- [Jodhpur Development Authority account of Khejarli](https://jda.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/raj/udh/development-authority/jda-jodhpur/pdf/SectorPlanEast1.pdf)
  records the 1730 resistance and 363 Bishnoi deaths. The course no longer turns the event into
  a single legal explanation for modern protection.
- [ICAR, khejri agroforestry in Rajasthan](https://www.icar.gov.in/en/greening-salt-impaired-lands-crop-diversification-success-story-pali-rajasthan)
  supports retaining khejri in fields and its ecological and economic value. Yield and nitrogen
  claims were narrowed to what arid agroforestry research supports.
- [CIPHET annual report on ker-sangri processing](https://ciphet.res.in/wp-content/uploads/files/CIPHET%20AR%202020%20%28IVth%20Draft%29_compressed.pdf)
  supports processing to reduce antinutritional compounds. Exact overnight rules and unsafe
  week-long room-temperature storage were removed; the recipe now gives refrigerated storage.
- [University of North Texas Jain studies food guidance](https://class.unt.edu/jain-studies/news/student-food-booth.html)
  and [Young Jains of America FAQ](https://www.yja.org/faq) support vegetarianism and the stricter
  avoidance of root vegetables. The text now distinguishes Jain observance from Marwari identity
  and states that lay practice varies.
- [Rajasthan urban-development canal history](https://lsg.urban.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/raj/udh/organizations/ruidp/SafeguardPolicies/IEE%20Nagaur%20Water%20Supply.pdf)
  and [RUIDP canal project record](https://jda.rajasthan.gov.in/content/dam/raj/udh/organizations/ruidp/SafeguardPolicies/Safeguard-IEEPhaseIII/IEE-PhaseIV/Water%20Supply%20and%20Sewerage%20in%20Sardarsharhar%20Updated%20August%2021.pdf)
  support the 1958 start, 445-kilometre main canal, Mohangarh terminus and 1986 completion. The
  claim that cooking continued unchanged was replaced by uneven agricultural and culinary change.

## Changes and conservation record

| Reading | Main corrections | Material retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Qualified water causation, corrected Chand Baori and ghee processing, removed universal yoghurt rules, narrowed crop rankings and treated Mathania heat as variable | Tanka water storage, ghee, kadhi, coriander, hing, kachri and clove |
| 2 | Replaced extreme millet claims, corrected bhujia production, made panchmel composition variable, removed universal roti and dairy claims | Bajra technique, bati and churma, dried pantry, mangodi, papad and moth bean |
| 3 | Marked the hunting-camp story as tradition, made kachri and dhungar optional, qualified soaking and cooking-water practices, added safe storage | Laal maas, Khejarli, ker sangri, gatte, ghevar and mawa kachori |
| 4 | Removed Rajput/Marwari binary, separated Jain rules from Marwari identity, qualified migration causes and corrected canal chronology | Mehrangarh, regional courts, Jain influence, merchant networks, famine memory and irrigation change |

Quizzes, spice cards, glossary entries and the three affected recipes now carry the same
qualifications in both languages. Panchmel remains a five-pulse recipe with one explicit mixture,
while the lesson and glossary explain that other mixtures are normal. The bilingual sheet
introduction now names dryland farming, court and trading histories, Jain practice and canal change.

## Review and verification

The English fact pass removed unsupported mechanisms, unsafe storage advice and community-wide
claims. The listener pass made origin stories and subjects explicit without relying on headings.
The Norwegian-first pass replaced literal calques, shortened long sentences and matched every
material qualification. The bilingual alignment pass confirmed list order, dish names and
dependent-content implications.

Prose lint leaves only inspected rhythm heuristics, with every sentence at or below the 35-word
ceiling. Structure, supporting-content, audio-freshness and transfer-test results are recorded in
the regional milestone in the transfer plan. Recording remains pending with the course-wide audio
stage, and no existing binary was overwritten.

## Sources and decisions

Sources accessed 21 September 2026:

- [Government of India, National Saffron Mission](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=68825):
  Pampore's share of Kashmir production and the 5,700-to-3,800-hectare and 3.13-to-2.50
  kg/ha declines published in 2010. The reading now dates these figures instead of presenting
  an undated decline.
- [FAO, Iran's qanat-based saffron system](https://www.fao.org/giahs/giahs-around-the-world/iran-qanat-based-saffron-system/en):
  Iran produces more than 90 percent of world saffron. Kashmir is described as a small,
  distinctive producer without implying that it supplies the world market.
- [Kashmiri Overseas Association cookbook, spices and ver](https://ikashmir.net/cookbook/doc/Chapters/chapter6.pdf):
  supports dried ver cakes and the broad Pandit/Muslim allium distinction. Removed the exact
  one-year freshness mechanism and distinguished garlic-based and asafoetida-based versions.
- [ICAR, traditional rice landraces](https://www.icar.gov.in/sites/default/files/2022-09/ICAR-News-January-March-2015.pdf)
  and [ICAR, Plant Genome Saviour awards](https://www.icar.gov.in/en/node/6197):
  support mushk budji as an aromatic landrace conserved by Kashmiri farmers and valued for
  ceremonies. Removed the claim that altitude turns rice short-grained and the unsupported
  farmer count and fifteen-year recovery window.
- [Journal of Ethnic Foods, wazwan meat products](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jef.2016.12.002)
  and [Sahapedia, wazwan](https://www.sahapedia.org/wazwan-culinary-legacy):
  support professional wazas, hand-pounded meat, the trami and the feast's social role.
  Thirty-six is retained as a grand ideal rather than a fixed count; gushtaba remains the
  conventional final meat course.
- [Government of India, Indian Food Heritage](https://nchm.nic.in/sites/default/files/2022-11/Indian_Food_Heritage.pdf):
  supports the central onion/garlic and asafoetida distinction between Muslim and Pandit
  traditions. The prose now calls this a tradition rather than an exception without variation.
- [Oxford-linked references collected under the rogan josh entry](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/rogan-josh)
  and the etymological discussion cited there: the exact derivation is not settled. The old
  course treated the Persian oil-and-boiling explanation as certain and blamed restaurant heat
  on mistranslation. Reading, quiz, glossary, recipe and the Bhopal cross-reference now state
  the uncertainty and separate colour from chilli heat.
- [UNESCO, Mughal Gardens in Kashmir](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5580):
  Shalimar's lower garden dates to around 1620, with earlier activity at the site; Nishat dates
  to the 1630s. The course no longer says that Jahangir created Shalimar in precisely 1619 or
  that chinars simply arrived with the Mughals.
- [Applied Water Science, Dal Lake land-use change](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13201-020-01300-5)
  and [Environmental Monitoring, Dal Lake water quality](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35220504/):
  support long-term water-quality pressure and an 11.86 percent decline in floating-garden area
  from 1980 to 2018. Replaced a broad century-long shrinkage claim with measured findings.
- [Encyclopaedic overview of the 1990 displacement and cited scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exodus_of_Kashmiri_Hindus):
  used only for the cautious statement that most Pandit families left or were forced to leave
  amid insurgency and violence around 1990. The course does not assign a single disputed cause
  or number.

## Review and verification

The English fact pass removed unsupported mechanisms, universal household claims and uncertain
etymologies stated as fact. The listener pass removed page references and catalogue openings,
and brought all readings below the 35-word sentence ceiling. The Norwegian-first pass removed
the same calques and rebuilt sentences around Norwegian subjects and word order. The final
alignment pass confirmed that qualifications, dates and recipe implications match.

Prose lint now reports only short rhythm heuristics. Each sequence was inspected; the sentences
vary in syntax and emphasis, so no further change was made merely to silence the heuristic.
Quiz check passes with 168 questions per language, spice check with 84 cards per language, and
recipe check with 69 bilingual recipes. All eight Himalaya reading tracks are stale after the
text edits; the old binaries were not overwritten. The introduction recording and HTTP playback
tests remain pending with the rest of production audio.

# Central India regional review

Completed 22 September 2026 as one four-reading bilingual batch. This ledger records the
research, conservation decisions, dependent-content reconciliation and verification used for
the internal regional gate. It does not authorize recording or publication.

## Sources and factual decisions

- [UNESCO, Buddhist Monuments at Sanchi](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/524) supports the
  Ashokan brick core, later enlargement and stone facing, carved gateways and Sanchi's status as
  one of the oldest extant Buddhist sanctuaries. The course no longer calls it India's oldest
  stone structure.
- [IGNCA, translations of Ashoka's inscriptions](https://ignca.gov.in/Asi_data/81727.pdf)
  supports the first Major Rock Edict's account of animals killed for the royal kitchen and
  Ashoka's stated intention to end the remaining practice. The reading confines this evidence to
  the court rather than inferring a change across the empire.
- [Chhattisgarh Legislative Assembly, state FAQ](https://cgvidhansabha.gov.in/english/faq.htm)
  establishes the state's formation on 1 November 2000. The modern boundary is no longer treated
  as an ancient culinary border.
- [Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Forest Rights Act](https://tribal.nic.in/FRA.aspx) and its
  [minor forest produce manual](https://tribal.nic.in/downloads/FRA/Minor%20Forest%20Produce/MFP-4%20operational%20manual%20of%20scheme%20mechanism%20for%20marketing%20MFP%2025.01.2014.pdf)
  support gatherers' rights and the economic importance of produce including mahua, tamarind,
  tendu and chironji. The readings now distinguish cultivation, gathering, household use and sale.
- [Bhopal district, official history](https://bhopal.mp.gov.in/about-district/) supports the
  city's establishment by Dost Mohammad Khan and the Begum era of 1819–1926.
  [Aligarh Muslim University](https://www.amu.ac.in/halls/begum-sultan-jahan-hall) supports Sultan
  Jahan's role as its first woman chancellor. The course now identifies regencies and interruptions
  instead of describing 107 years of uninterrupted female succession.
- [Bastar district, culture and heritage](https://bastar.gov.in/en/tourism/culture-heritage/) and
  [Incredible India, Bastar Dussehra](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/chhattisgarh/bastar-dussehra)
  support a festival of roughly 75 days centred on Danteshwari and local deities rather than the
  burning of Ravana.
- [Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Authority, citation book](https://plantauthority.gov.in/sites/default/files/citation-book-2021.pdf)
  supports Richharia's work collecting and conserving rice landraces. The course now describes a
  collection in the thousands, often reported around 19,000, rather than claiming that 20,000
  varieties remain under cultivation today.

## Changes and conservation record

| Reading | Main corrections | Material retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Removed fixed proportions of gathered food, claims that chironji is never cultivated, universal mahua practices and a waste-product story for poppy seed; identified the garam masala as the course blend | Poppy seed, chironji, mahua, tamarind, aamat and forest-produce rights |
| 2 | Replaced a clean wheat/rice border with an overlapping crop map, qualified soil-moisture and rice-landrace claims, and added safe cooling guidance for cooked-rice dishes | Malwa wheat and gram, Chhattisgarh rice, poha, bafla, basi and Bastar markets |
| 3 | Removed a fixed steam-versus-fry divide, varied jiravan and Ratlami sev formulas, made less-sweet corn a preference, and treated Bhopal's court food as one strand of the city | Indori poha, sev, jiravan, bhutte ka kees, dal bafla, chila and korma |
| 4 | Corrected Sanchi and the Begum chronology, qualified donation and trade claims, removed essentialising descriptions of Bastar communities and medical claims for chapda | Ashoka's edict, Sanchi, the four Begums, Bastar Dussehra and forest rights |

The quizzes now test qualified distinctions and food-safe handling. Glossary entries and spice
cards describe variable formulas and sourcing without absolutes. Recipes label their own methods
as versions, distinguish dal bafla from related dal bati without ranking them, give workable corn
adaptations and frame the korma as one Bhopali style. The bilingual regional introduction now
brings together Malwa, Chhattisgarh, Bhopal and Bastar without treating them as sealed cuisines.

## Review and verification

The English fact pass removed unsupported totals, origins, binaries and biological claims. The
listener pass shortened all four readings and clarified transitions. The Norwegian-first pass
removed literal and categorical constructions while preserving the same uncertainty. The
bilingual alignment pass confirmed dates, names, safety guidance, dish distinctions and list order.

Prose lint passes with every sentence at or below the 35-word ceiling. Wiring, quiz, spice, recipe
and credit checks pass, as do all seven offline transfer tests. The site build completes at 19.7 MB
with no recordings in the worktree. The freshness check reports the expected 64 stale scripts:
eight for each of the eight completed regions. Recording remains pending with the course-wide
audio stage, and no existing binary was overwritten.

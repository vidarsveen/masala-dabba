# Bengal and the east regional review

Completed 22 September 2026 as one four-reading bilingual batch. This ledger records the
research, conservation decisions, dependent-content reconciliation and verification used for
the internal regional gate. It does not authorize recording or publication.

## Sources and factual decisions

- [FSSAI food-borne illness guidance](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2021/10/6177ebdb6d980Direction_Food_Bourne_Illness_26_10_2021.pdf)
  identifies cooked and fried rice held at ambient temperature as a common vehicle for
  *Bacillus cereus*. The course now recommends a tested fermentation or prompt cooling,
  refrigeration and chilled water rather than an uncontrolled overnight rest.
- [Agricultural Statistics at a Glance 2024–25](https://nccd.gov.in/uploads/Agricultural_Statisitcs_at_a_Glance_2024_25_11da3cd37d.pdf)
  places West Bengal among India's leading rice producers but not first in that year. The reading
  no longer presents a changing annual ranking as a permanent fact.
- [ICAR on hilsa restoration in the upper Ganga](https://www.icar.gov.in/en/after-decades-hilsa-returns-upper-ganga-remarkable-story-river-restoration-and-ecological)
  supports the importance of migration routes and river barriers. The reading now centres
  juveniles, spawning fish, fishing pressure and connected rivers rather than origin claims.
- [Odisha Tourism, Jagannath Temple](https://odishatourism.gov.in/content/tourism/en/discover/attractions/temples-monuments/jagannath-temple.html)
  supports cooking offerings over wood fires in stacked earthen pots. The temple kitchen remains
  a major institution but no longer stands in for all Odia food or carries a world-largest claim.
- The Indian GI Registry entries for
  [Banglar Rasogolla](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/533) and
  [Odisha Rasagola](https://search.ipindia.gov.in/GIRPublicSearch/Application/Details/612)
  establish separate registered products and territories. The course explains that registration
  does not settle who invented rasgulla.
- [NIFTEM's paneer processing report](https://niftem-t.ac.in/pmfme/dpr-paneer.pdf) notes an
  acid-curdled milk preparation in the twelfth-century *Manasollasa*. This is evidence against a
  simple claim that the technique first arrived in India with the Portuguese, while it does not
  by itself trace modern chhana sweets.
- [West Bengal's cultural repository on Howrah Bridge](https://icad.wb.gov.in/wb-admin/uploads/archive/9142420605851714731711.pdf)
  supports the 1943 commissioning and riveted construction used in the introduction.
- [Jharkhand's official state profile](https://www.jharkhand.gov.in/home/AboutState) establishes
  state formation on 15 November 2000. Government-hosted research on
  [non-timber forest products](https://jbb.jharkhand.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/362.-NON-TIMBER-FOREST-PRODUCTS-NTFPs-SUPPORTING-FOOD-SECURITY-IN-TRIBAL-JHARKHAND.pdf)
  supports their importance without reducing the state's many communities to a single forest diet.

## Changes and conservation record

| Reading | Main corrections | Material retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Made panch phoron, kasundi and shukto formulas variable; removed universal tempering rules, a false mustard-enzyme explanation and an unsupported opium-waste origin story for posto | Whole-seed tempering, mustard oil and paste, nigella, posto and kasundi |
| 2 | Separated delta lowlands from the four-state map, corrected the rice ranking, qualified formal meal order and replaced unsafe room-temperature rice advice | Rice seasons, panta and pakhala, gobindobhog, litti chokha and Puri temple cooking |
| 3 | Replaced fish-washing folklore with safe handling, centred hilsa conservation, made jhol and kosha families rather than fixed formulas, and explained GI scope accurately | Ilish, shorshe preparations, macher jhol, kosha mangsho, chhana sweets and mishti doi |
| 4 | Qualified Portuguese language and cheesemaking claims, retained pre-Portuguese acid-curd evidence, labelled potato stories undocumented and removed essentialising descriptions of Jharkhand | Hooghly contact, Wajid Ali Shah at Metiabruz, Kolkata biryani, Jagannath and eastern state histories |

The quizzes now test qualified distinctions and safe rice handling. Glossary entries and spice
cards describe variable formulas and ingredient identities without universal rules. Recipes label
their methods as course versions, require edible-labelled mustard oil, keep marinated meat chilled,
and treat food-safe clay as optional for mishti doi. The English and Norwegian dish lists retain
the same order and vegetarian flags.

## Review and verification

The English fact pass removed unsupported rankings, origins, binaries and universal practices. The
listener pass clarified the four-state scope and made transitions work without headings. The
Norwegian-first pass removed categorical translations and time-sensitive shop claims. The bilingual
alignment pass confirmed dates, names, safety guidance, uncertainty and list order.

Prose lint has no sentence above the 35-word ceiling; remaining rhythm and catalogue heuristics were
inspected in context. Wiring, quiz, spice, recipe and credit checks pass, as do all seven offline
transfer tests. The site build completes at 19.6 MB with no recordings in the worktree. The freshness
check reports the expected 72 stale scripts: eight for each of the nine completed regions. Recording
remains pending with the course-wide audio stage, and no existing binary was overwritten.

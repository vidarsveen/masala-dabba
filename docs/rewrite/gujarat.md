# Gujarat regional review

Completed 22 September 2026 as one four-reading bilingual batch. This ledger records the
research, conservation decisions, dependent-content reconciliation and verification used for
the internal regional gate. It does not authorize recording or publication.

## Sources and factual decisions

- [Spices Board India, asafoetida](https://www.indianspices.com/node/9.html) identifies Afghanistan
  and Iran as major suppliers to India. The reading no longer treats those two countries as the
  source of nearly every gram or makes unsupported digestive claims.
- [Government of Gujarat, Gujarat Prohibition Act 1949](https://cpahmedabad.gujarat.gov.in/Upload/prohibition1949.pdf)
  confirms the current statutory name and the prohibitions with legislated exceptions. The course
  no longer presents chaas as a direct consequence of prohibition.
- [Gujarat Tourism, Gujarati cuisines](https://gujarattourism.com/gujarati-cuisines.html?cid=14)
  supports the varied breads, thali components, mishthaan served with the meal, seasonal undhiyu
  and broad farsan repertoire. Restaurant service and tray layout are now presented as variable.
- [Gujarat Tourism, thepla](https://gujarattourism.com/gujarati-cuisines/thepla.html) supports wheat,
  gram flour and fenugreek as a common form while documenting bottle-gourd and radish variants.
  Unsafe claims of three or four days at room temperature were removed from the lesson, glossary
  and recipe; the course now tells readers to cool before packing and refrigerate longer storage.
- [UNESCO tentative list, Lothal](https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/5918) describes the
  Harappan settlement as a port town on the Bhogava river. The course preserves the long-running
  debate about the brick basin rather than calling it the uncontested oldest dock in the world.
- [Journal of Archaeological Science, 2024 Lothal landscape study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0305440324001146)
  reconstructs an ancient connection to the Sabarmati system and strengthens the dock interpretation
  while documenting earlier reservoir readings. The lesson now reflects both the newer evidence
  and the history of dispute.
- [British Library, East India Company factory records](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/036-000178141)
  and the [India Office factory-record list](https://tamildigitallibrary.in/assets/docs/uploads/primary_files/book/TVA_BOK_0046310_List_of_Factory_Records.pdf)
  document English visits to Surat and the January 1613 firman. The false claim that Surat was the
  Company's first Indian factory was replaced with what the record establishes.
- [International School for Jain Studies survey](https://isjs.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/National-Report.pdf)
  records eating before sunset as an important Jain practice while showing varied observance among
  contemporary youth. The course now distinguishes religious discipline from universal behaviour.
- [Encyclopaedia Iranica, early Parsi history](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/parsi-communities-i-early-history/)
  identifies the late-sixteenth-century <em>Qissa-i Sanjan</em> as a cautious narrative source and
  the sugar-in-milk episode as oral tradition. [Australian National University research](https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/items/2169b06e-06a5-4bc9-9070-fb0a0728b5f7)
  establishes that the famous episode is absent from the surviving poem. The quiz now tests that
  distinction instead of attributing the legend to the <em>Qissa</em>.
- [Encyclopaedia Iranica, Iranshah](https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/iransah/) traces the fire's
  remembered Sanjan origin and later movement to Udvada. Claims of an independently proven,
  uninterrupted flame were replaced by the distinction between Parsi tradition and later records.
- [UNESCO, Rani ki Vav](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/922) dates the monument to the eleventh
  century and describes seven levels, water management, the inverted-temple design and more than
  five hundred principal sculptures. Unsupported precision about the 1060s and excavation decade
  was removed.

## Changes and conservation record

| Reading | Main corrections | Material retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Replaced statewide flavour rules with regional and household variation, qualified dhana-jeera and vaghar, corrected hing sourcing and removed unsupported digestive claims | Sweet-sour-hot balance, coriander and cumin, hing, jaggery, kokum, mustard and Uttarayan sesame sweets |
| 2 | Reframed tray layout and service as restaurant conventions, corrected kadhi stability, removed unsafe thepla storage and separated prohibition law from beverage custom | Thal and katori, farsan, kadhi, rotli, thepla, chaas and sweets served with the meal |
| 3 | Removed the false frying-versus-steaming binary, allowed naming overlap between dhokla and khaman, distinguished undhiyu from related umbhadiyu and qualified festival universals | Fermented batters, khandvi technique, handvo, seasonal vegetables, muthiya and fafda-jalebi |
| 4 | Updated the Lothal debate, corrected the 1613 Company claim, removed deterministic merchant and vegetarian explanations, varied Jain observance, separated milk-and-sugar legend from the Qissa and corrected Rani ki Vav | Gujarat's ports, ahimsa, Sanjan and Udvada traditions, and stepwell water engineering |

Quizzes, glossary entries, spice cards and affected recipe notes now carry the same qualifications
in both languages. The khandvi recipe no longer invents a protein-gel mechanism; the thepla recipe
gives safe storage; and the undhiyu recipe treats umbhadiyu as a related village preparation and
adds prompt refrigeration for leftovers. The bilingual introduction now names the forces shaping
the cuisine without erasing Gujaratis who eat meat, fish or eggs.

## Review and verification

The English fact pass removed absolutes, causal shortcuts, unsafe storage and one false source
attribution. The listener pass shortened all four readings, removed internal page references and
made disputed subjects explicit in spoken prose. The Norwegian-first pass replaced literal and
mannered constructions while preserving qualifications. The bilingual alignment pass confirmed
method distinctions, religious-practice caveats, historical uncertainty and list order.

Prose lint leaves only inspected rhythm heuristics, with every sentence at or below the 35-word
ceiling. Structure, supporting-content, audio-freshness and transfer-test results are recorded in
the regional milestone in the transfer plan. Recording remains pending with the course-wide audio
stage, and no existing binary was overwritten.

Punjab & Delhi.

A fresh roti arrives while the last one is still being eaten. Across Punjab's plains, wheat and dairy shape the daily meal, while a tandoor gives bread a blistered surface and meat the smell of smoke. This region brings Punjab together with Haryana, Chandigarh and Delhi. Its kitchens connect winter mustard greens and slow-cooked dal with the restaurants refugees opened after Partition in 1947. In Amritsar, the langar at the Golden Temple offers another way to share a meal: people sit together and receive the same food.

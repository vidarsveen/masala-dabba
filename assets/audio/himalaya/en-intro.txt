Kashmir & the Himalaya.

Saffron flowers on the karewa terraces at Pampore, while short-grained rice carries the gravies of the Kashmir valley. Muslim and Pandit cooks share dish names but follow different rules for onion and garlic. A wedding wazwan unfolds course by course on a copper platter, ending with gushtaba. Beyond the valley, the terrain rises through Himachal and Uttarakhand into Ladakh. Millets, barley and salted butter tea then mark different crops, climates and long connections across the mountains.
